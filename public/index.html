Maturity Level Framework

Five-tier progression model for process excellence

Level 1Initial/Ad Hoc

Characteristics: Processes are unpredictable, poorly controlled, and reactive. Success depends on individual heroics.

Key Indicators: Inconsistent results, lack of documentation, informal procedures, high rework.

Level 2Repeatable/Managed

Characteristics: Some processes are established with basic discipline. Requirements are captured and tracked.

Key Indicators: Standard procedures documented, basic project management, some metrics collection, cost and schedule management.

Level 3Defined/Standardized

Characteristics: Processes are documented, standardized, and proactively managed. Organization-wide standards exist.

Key Indicators: Process standards documented, tailoring guidelines, process training, process assurance activities, continuous monitoring.

Level 4Quantitatively Managed

Characteristics: Processes are measured and controlled quantitatively. Significant variation is understood and managed.

Key Indicators: Statistical process control, quantitative process management, detailed metrics and analysis, performance baselines.

Level 5Optimizing

Characteristics: Focus on continuous process improvement and innovation. Organization proactively optimizes its processes.

Key Indicators: Continuous improvement culture, pilot innovations, rapid deployment of new technologies, optimization metrics.

Gap Assessment Framework

Structured methodology to identify and address process improvements

The Gap Assessment Process

1

Define Current State

Assess the existing maturity of your processes through interviews, document review, and observation.

2

Establish Target State

Define desired maturity levels based on strategic goals, industry standards, and organizational capability.

3

Identify Gaps

Compare current and target states to pinpoint specific areas requiring improvement and prioritize them.

4

Root Cause Analysis

Investigate underlying causes of gaps using frameworks like 5-Why, fishbone analysis, or capability mapping.

5

Develop Roadmap

Create a prioritized action plan with timelines, resources, ownership, and success metrics.

6

Monitor & Sustain

Track progress through KPIs, conduct follow-up assessments, and refine the roadmap based on results.
