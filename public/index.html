What Is a Maturity Assessment?

A maturity assessment evaluates how advanced an organization, team, or process is in a given domain, and exactly what's needed to reach the next level.

Instead of a vague "how are we doing?", it gives you a structured, benchmarked answer: a defined scale from ad-hoc and reactive to optimized and self-improving, backed by evidence from your own answers rather than guesswork.

Types of Maturity Assessments

The same underlying method, applied to different domains.

Capability Maturity (CMM)

Measures how disciplined and repeatable your software or engineering processes are. It's the industry-standard model for development capability.

Security Maturity

A first look at how mature your cybersecurity posture really is, so you can build a protection strategy grounded in your actual gaps.

Business Process Maturity

Builds a framework around your core processes, such as sales, operations, and service delivery, so you know exactly where to focus improvement.

Compliance & Standards

Benchmarks you against a named framework, such as HIPAA, SOC 2, ISO 27001, or PCI DSS, with a gap-by-gap readiness picture.

What Are Maturity Levels?

A maturity level shows how far along you are in a domain. Most assessments use somewhere between 3 and 10 levels. Here's a typical 4-level example:

1
Incidental

Activities happen, but mostly by accident rather than strategic intent.

2
Intentional

The need is recognized and pockets of good practice exist, but nothing is automated or company-wide yet.

3
Integrated

Well-coordinated, automated initiatives executed with clear strategic intent across the organization.

4
Optimized

Fully embedded in the culture: coordinated, automated, and continuously streamlined.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should an organization run a maturity assessment? ▾
Anytime you need an objective baseline before committing resources. Common triggers are ahead of a transformation initiative, before pursuing a certification, after a leadership change, or simply on a recurring cadence to track whether past improvements actually stuck.
What's the difference between a maturity assessment and a diagnostic? ▾
They overlap heavily and the terms are often used interchangeably. If there's a distinction, it's emphasis: a "diagnostic" tends to focus narrowly on finding what's wrong right now, while a maturity assessment also places you on a defined scale and shows the path to the next level: current state, target state, and the gap between them.
Does MaturityAssess use AI for report generation? ▾
Yes. Claude generates the assessment questions themselves (tailored to your industry and standard, not a generic template), scores your responses, and writes the executive report and roadmap. It's AI end to end, not a static form with canned advice bolted on.
Can I use my own maturity model or framework? ▾
The platform ships with 8 industries and 20+ standard frameworks out of the box, and new industries, standards, and assessment templates can be added without touching any code. If you need a fully custom model built around your own framework, talk to our team. That's exactly the kind of thing a facilitated engagement is for.
Is the assessment free? ▾
Yes. The free tier gives you an instant scorecard. The full personalized report with a downloadable .docx is also free right now, capped at 2 per day per email while we're in early access.
Can I upload existing policy documents instead of answering questions? ▾
