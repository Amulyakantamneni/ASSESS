Security Assessments

A security assessment evaluates your organization's technical and organizational controls against a recognized security framework — helping you understand your real risk posture before an incident, audit, or customer security review forces the issue.

Frameworks We Assess Against

  • SOC 2 — trust-services criteria for SaaS and service providers
  • ISO 27001 — information security management systems
  • HITRUST — common security framework for healthcare
  • NIST 800-53 / FedRAMP — federal information security controls

What Gets Evaluated

  • Access control and identity management practices
  • Data protection, encryption, and retention policies
  • Incident response and monitoring capability
  • Vendor and third-party risk management
