What is CMM?

The Capability Maturity Model (CMM) is a framework for evaluating and improving how consistently an organization executes its processes — originally developed for software engineering, now applied broadly across process, security, and compliance assessments.

The Five Maturity Levels

1
Initial / Ad Hoc

Processes are unpredictable and reactive. Success depends on individual effort rather than repeatable process.

2
Repeatable / Managed

Basic project management and process discipline exist. Requirements are captured and tracked.

3
Defined / Standardized

Processes are documented, standardized, and proactively managed across the organization.

4
Quantitatively Managed

Processes are measured and controlled with quantitative data. Variation is understood.

5
Optimizing

Continuous improvement is embedded in culture. The organization proactively refines its own processes.

Why Organizations Use Maturity Assessments

  • Benchmark current capability against an objective, industry-recognized scale
  • Identify specific, prioritized gaps rather than vague improvement areas
  • Build a credible business case for process investment
  • Track improvement over time with repeatable, comparable assessments
