What is CMM?

The Capability Maturity Model (CMM) is a framework for evaluating and improving how consistently an organization executes its processes. It was originally developed for software engineering and is now applied broadly across process, security, and compliance assessments.

The Five Maturity Levels

1
Initial / Ad Hoc

Processes are unpredictable and reactive. Success depends on individual effort rather than a repeatable process.

2
Repeatable / Managed

Basic project management and process discipline exist. Requirements are captured and tracked.

3
Defined / Standardized

Processes are documented, standardized, and proactively managed across the organization.

4
Quantitatively Managed

Processes are measured and controlled with quantitative data, and variation is understood.

5
Optimizing

Continuous improvement is embedded in the culture. The organization proactively refines its own processes.

Why Organizations Use It

📏

An Objective Benchmark

A recognized scale to measure capability against, instead of relying on gut feel.

🎯

Prioritized Gaps

💼

🔁
